FAWLTY TOWERS CHARACTER GUIDE

BASIL FAWLTY (John Cleese)
- Tall, thin, manic energy, suppressed rage always bubbling under surface
- Desperately aspires to sophistication but is deeply insecure
- Sycophantic to "upper class" guests, dismissive to working class
- Catchphrases: "Don't mention the war!", nervous laugh, "He's from Barcelona"
- Physical comedy: The walk, the exasperated looks to camera, strangling Manuel
- Deep fear of Sybil, tries to hide things from her

SYBIL FAWLTY (Prunella Scales)
- Sharp, controlling, sees through Basil's schemes immediately
- Famous laugh: "Ahhh ha ha ha ha!"
- On phone constantly to Audrey, discussing operations and complaints
- Runs the hotel competently when Basil isn't interfering
- Catchphrase: "Basil!" (warning tone), "I know, I know"
- Eye rolls, withering looks, cold fury more terrifying than shouting

MANUEL (Andrew Sachs)
- Spanish waiter, struggles with English, tries desperately to help
- Catchphrases: "Que?", "I know nothing", "Is not my fault"
- Loyal to Mr. Fawlty despite constant abuse
- Physical comedy: Falling, misunderstanding, getting hit
- Innocent, childlike, genuinely wants to do well

POLLY SHERMAN (Connie Booth)
- Voice of reason, covers for Basil's schemes (reluctantly)
- Art student, intelligent, long-suffering
- Often dragged into increasingly absurd situations
- Mediates between Basil and guests
- Dry wit, exasperated expressions

EPISODE PREMISE: "The Wellness Weekend"

A wellness retreat company has booked the entire hotel for a "Digital Detox & Mindfulness Weekend."
The guests are stressed London executives who've paid premium prices to be pampered.

Complications:
- Basil sees "wellness" as new age nonsense but loves the premium prices
- The yoga instructor is a pompous fraud who Basil despises but must kowtow to
- Manuel has been told to serve only herbal tea, can't understand what's wrong with normal tea
- The hotel's ancient plumbing chooses this weekend to make terrible noises
- One guest is a restaurant critic in disguise
- Sybil has secretly signed up for the weekend herself

Classic Fawlty escalation: Small misunderstandings snowball into catastrophe
END_DOCUMENT

BBC COMEDY SCRIPT FORMAT

SCENE HEADING: INT. or EXT., LOCATION, TIME OF DAY
Example: INT. FAWLTY TOWERS LOBBY - DAY

CHARACTER NAME centred, CAPS
Dialogue under character name, regular case
(Parentheticals in brackets, lowercase, for tone/action)

Stage directions in italics or (brackets)
Keep directions minimal - trust actors

TIMING: 30 minutes broadcast = ~35 pages
Each scene: 2-5 pages typical
Cold open before titles: 2-3 minutes

STRUCTURE (for Fawlty Towers):
- Cold open establishing the situation
- Titles
- Basil's scheme begins
- First complication
- Things appear to resolve
- Much worse complication
- Frantic Act 3 with escalating chaos
- Resolution (usually ironic punishment for Basil)
