SCENARIO: Office Secret Santa Drama

OPTIONS:

- Reset and Redo: Everyone returns gifts, draw names again, strict £15 limit
- Charity Pivot: Donate all gifts to charity shop, do group donation instead
- Public Shaming: Make overspender and regifter apologise to the group
- Sliding Scale: New rule - £10-20 range, receipts required, no regifting
