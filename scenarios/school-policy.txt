- 73% of students have smartphones

OPTIONS:

- Pouch System: Phones locked in signal-blocking pouches during school hours, students keep them
- Lesson-Only Ban: Current policy but with stricter enforcement and consequences
- Phone-Free Zones: Designated areas where phones allowed (cafeteria, outdoor), banned elsewhere
