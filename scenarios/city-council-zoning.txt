LOCATION: Town Hall - Council Chamber

SCENARIO: Town Council - The Riverside Development

PUBLIC INFO:
- Proposed 200-unit apartment complex on former industrial site
- Site has been vacant for 15 years, currently an eyesore
- Developer offering £1.5M in community benefits (Section 106)
- Traffic study shows significant impact on Oak Street
- 12 affordable housing units included (6% of total)
- Public comment period received 200 objections, 50 letters of support
- Planning committee needs 4 votes to approve (7 members total)

OPTIONS:
- Approve As Proposed: Accept developer's plan with current community benefits package
- Approve With Conditions: Require 20% affordable housing, additional traffic mitigation, green space
- Deny Application: Reject development, site remains vacant, look for alternative proposals
- Send Back to Committee: Request redesign with reduced density (150 units), more study time
- Approve Reduced Plan: Allow 150 units, developer keeps community benefits commitment
